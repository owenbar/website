Getting started in Ireland

A good way to get started with training in Ireland is by attending one of the Irish Maths Trust Enrichment programmes. The Irish Mathematical Olympiad is Ireland’s largest competition, and the top 25 students here will be invited to the IMO team-selection camps. As a starting point, I recommend doing past IrMO papers under timed conditions - make sure to always write out full proofs!

Camps

Two programmes I’d recommend to any Irish student who enjoys maths beyond the classroom. Both are competitive entry, so apply early.

Maths Beyond Limits (website)

A ten-day international maths camp for secondary school students. I went three times, to Bosnia and Herzegovina and to Poland, and gave talks on Benford’s Law and the Prisoner’s Dilemma. [What you got out of it.]

Program on AI and Reasoning (PAIR) (website)

A workshop in the UK on how modern AI systems work and how they relate to theories of the mind, covering information theory, neural network architectures and neuroscience. I attended in 2024 after a three-round application. [What you got out of it.]
